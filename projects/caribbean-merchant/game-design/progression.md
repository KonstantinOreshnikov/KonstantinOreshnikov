# Progression and player motivation

The v1.0 career extends the initial sandbox with:

- **16 stages/ranks**, from an unknown sailor/trader to a Caribbean legend.
- **82 achievements** across trade, travel, combat, industry, contracts, wealth, city development and rival activities.
- **Three victory paths**: commercial, maritime and government/status progression.
- One-time achievement and promotion rewards; duplicate claims are guarded.

The player's preferred experience is **gradual progression without administrative busywork**. Automated factories finance personal naval exploration. City buildings, reputation and contracts offer alternatives to endlessly repeating the highest-margin trade route.

**What is deliberately not included:** automatic live multiplayer, captain-controlled parallel convoys or an exhaustive political AI.

For canonical game behavior, inspect the released [standalone HTML](../play/CaribbeanMerchant_v1.0.html). This page is explanatory design documentation, not an executable specification.
