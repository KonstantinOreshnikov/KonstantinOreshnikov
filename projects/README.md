These pages are **sanitized case studies and marked development concepts**. Chess Odyssey's online multiplayer and cloud-backed account E2E remain unfinished; Lord of Ultima-inspired work stays at design/research. No production database dumps, individual records, answer keys, confidential training materials, secrets, tokens, internal service URLs, company-owned code, or privileged security procedures are shared. Figures describe different project states and should not be conflated with active users, deployment readiness or completed QA.
