# v0.5 Solar System — implementation scope

Eight selectable scenario profiles, three modes, schema 5 and migrations from v1-v4. Mars retains exact previous environmental tick and measured MOLA rendering. Other worlds use procedural geometry/colors and fictional site elevations; not measured geography. Game model initial values approximate NASA temperatures/pressures where available, with fictional water/biomass/hazard/stock/funding values. [NASA temperatures](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/temperatures-across-our-solar-system/), [planetary fact sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/index.html), [Venus pressure](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/venusfact.html), [Titan facts](https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/moons/titan/facts/), [Voyager fact sheet](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/fact-sheet/). Venus 9200 kPa and 464 C, vacuum and cold bodies supported without Mars clipping. No actual hydrocarbon mass model or species simulation; hazard indices are game abstractions. Mercury high solar/thermal exposure; Moon atmospheric escape; Venus heat/pressure; Europa radiation/ice; Titan weak sun/hydrocarbon hazard; Pluto escape/low energy; Earth fictional ecological crisis. Hazard mitigation tied to world conditions, infrastructure and research; non-Earth hazard adds upkeep. Earth begins three inherited regions and population 240, climate 27 C, hazard 85, biomass 30; conservation, protection, resource management and relocation of 10 people at cost. Failure below 150 population is irreversible within campaign (manual restore/new game available). Earth victory population >=270, hazard <=10, biomass >=70, temp 12–19 C, protected regions at least level 2, supplies >=60, other shared goals; 15 stable years rather than 5. All values fictional; not a prediction. Earth rescue funding 40/year plus population income (other worlds 20) supports inherited infrastructure. Initial balancing is provisional, difficulty ranking not certified. All 24 fresh model routes reached victory after correcting Earth strategy to maintain oxygen across 15-year streak, no final-state injection. Model runs use procedural fallback; real MOLA packaged browser gate separate. Native editorial QA, real device, independent playtest, realistic world-specific maps and detailed climate chemistry not verified. No release or merge.
