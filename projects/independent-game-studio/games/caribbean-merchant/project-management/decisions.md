# Design decision log

These are user-approved design directions recorded during iterative work — not retroactively invented architecture meeting minutes.

| ID | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D-01 | **One active player ship**, trade in the old one. | Managing and escorting fleets would defeat the desired relaxing gameplay. |
| D-02 | **Auto-sale with retention rules**. | Factories in many cities must not require personal collection trips. |
| D-03 | **All ten goods available to manufacture in every city**. | Intentional convenience departure from original PC specialization. |
| D-04 | **Warehouse expansion to 300 units per good**. | The original 150 limit constrained long unattended production. |
| D-05 | **Atomic contract loading** at acceptance. | Human playtest revealed missed deadlines caused by traveling with no cargo. |
| D-06 | **True geographical relief** and legible port names. | First visual redesign failed user acceptance. |
| D-07 | **Money/hold visible while trading**. | Avoid scrolling to verify affordability and cargo capacity. |
| D-08 | **RU/EN/AZ in a single HTML file**. | Quick sharing across language audiences, offline accessibility. |
| D-09 | **Long-term career, achievements and city projects**. | Provide goals beyond endlessly increasing treasury. |
| D-10 | **Separate original mechanics from inspired additions**. | Avoid falsely claiming a modern convenience feature was in a 2000s mobile game. |
